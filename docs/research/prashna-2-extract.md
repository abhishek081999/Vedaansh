# Introduction to Prashna

## Why prashna

The biggest reason for failed astrological predictions is undoubtedly the non-availability of an accurate birth horoscope. In the earlier times either the watches were not available or even where they were available, the botheration of noting down the correct time of birth was not taken. Majority of child births took place at home and the doctor or midwife obviously were too busy to save the life of the child and the mother rather than to ignore them for noting down the time of birth. Only after they became free from other duties, they noted down the time, guessing it to be 10 or 15 minutes or more earlier. Further, there are different view points with regard to the time of birth also, which include the time of first cry, the time of first appearance of the head, the severing of the umbilical cord or the bhusparsh - the first touch of the body with the ground. All these reasons lead us to a situation where the accuracy of birth time remains debatable. The present day births in hospitals also reveal a similar story.

In some cases there is total confusion with regard to the availability of birth details of day, month or even year of birth. We often hear that the only fact remembered by the elders in the family is that it was too hot and rainy season was about to commence or the Moon was half or full or nearly so. This again leads us to the majority of horoscopes which are based on approximations only. There have been various social evils attached to the marriage of a girl child like dowry and the role of unscrupulous astrologers who would, for mere monetary gains, find faults in the girls horoscopes in matching and recommend costly pujas or propitiation of planets for successful culmination of marriage. Due to these factors in the past, many families stopped noting down the birth details of the girl child, which itself led to the gap in knowledge of astrology because of absence of horoscopes.

Even in cases where birth time is noted, its accuracy cannot be guaranteed for the reasons enumerated above. The existing methods of rectification of birth time are neither effective nor popular. On top of all this we have cases where the birth details are not known at all. Can such persons be denied the benefits of astrology. There are situations of urgencies or emergencies when there is no time to either cast the horoscope or to retrieve it due to lack of time. In all such cases prashna kundali gives amazingly accurate results. This book therefore deals with this wonderful field of astrology which popularly goes under the name of prashna or Horary astrology.

## Astrology and prashna

Astrology is one of the six parts called vedanga. These are shiksha, kalpa, nirukta, chhanda, vyakaran and jyotisha. Jyotisha was further divided into three skandhas namely ganita, samhita and hora. Ganita, the first skandha, deals with astronomy and mathematical astrology. They are compiled into different sidhantas which were as many as eighteen in number in the ancient times. Samhita, the second skandha, deals with natural phenomenon like earthquakes, weather, famines, epidemics etc. Hora or jataka, the third skandha, deals with the natal predictive astrology.

Subsequent developments in this field led to astrology being classified into six skandhas instead of the earlier three. These were ganita, samhita, hora, shakun, muhurat and prashna. Shakun or omens, the fourth skandha, deals with the effects of certain occurrences or phenomenon on future events. Muhurat, the fifth skandha, deals with finding out the auspicious moment for doing any work based on nakshatra, tithi, vaar etc. and related to the natal as well as transit position of planets. Prashna, the sixth skandha, deals with casting of horoscopes for the time of query, event or thought, and forms the subject matter of this book. No skandha can be dealt in isolation. Astrology is a complete blend of all the parts or skandhas stated above.

## Birth horoscope and prashna horoscope

The importance of birth horoscope cannot be underestimated. Any event promised in the birth chart and repeated in the prashna chart is sure to occur. The reverse however, may not be true. The inter relationship of birth chart and prashna chart also reveals the status of past and present karmas. The birth chart is the map of heavens at the time of birth and indicates the prarabdha or accumulated karmas allowed for this life. Benefic influences on birth chart indicates good karmas of the past life whereas malefic influences indicate evil karmas. This aspect is explained in detail in the chapter on karma and evil spirits.

A good dasha in the birth chart and a bad period in the prashna chart indicates evil karmas of present life which will predominate. A bad dasha in birth chart and good period in prashna chart indicates good karmas in present life which again will predominate. A good or bad period in both the charts will indicate the relevant good or bad dridha karmas of the past birth.

As in the analysis of divisional charts, a relevant divisional chart can only supplement the readings of the birth chart and can never supplant it. A promise absent in birth chart and present in the divisional chart may not fructify. This is so because in the relative vimshopak strength of different charts given by Maharishi Parashara, the birth chart is given the maximum strength out of a total of 20 points whereas the other divisional charts have a comparatively much lesser strength. Similarly the prashna chart too can only supplement the birth chart. However there are innumerable areas in prediction which can best be answered by prashna only.

Hence, whenever a birth chart is available, in addition to the prashna chart it is prudent to see the birth chart also. In cases where birth chart is not available, the prashna chart not only reveals about the present query but it can also be read as the birth chart giving amazingly accurate results.

## Specific use of prashna kundali

Even where accurate birth chart is available, there are many areas of knowing immediate future or solving the immediate problems and anxieties of the querist which can be dealt only by a prashna chart and the role of birth chart can at best be stated to be supplementary. For example, in case of theft the recovery of stolen property, the appearance of the thief etc. In case of missing persons whether he is alive or dead, his present state of well being, whether captivity or not, chances of his release. In case of illness the likelihood of recovery, whether the medicine is appropriate, whether the diagnosis is correct. In case of marriage whether the person will get the girl of his choice, the prospects and auspiciousness of marriage. In case of progeny whether the woman is pregnant, what will be the sex of the child. In innumerable queries like this it is only the prashna chart which can be relied upon to give amazingly accurate results. The importance of this field of specialisation of astrology can be gauged from the fact that no astrologer can succeed in making predictions without deeply involving himself in prashna. In Hindu mythology the creator of the universe, Lord Brahma, once approached the preserver Lord Vishnu and expressed his desire to learn prashna to solve the anxieties pertaining to the immediate future. The origin of prashna shastra is hence as ancient.

## The basis of prashna

The birth chart is constructed for the precise moment of birth of a child. Similarly the prashna chart is constructed for the moment when the querist approaches the astrologer for knowing his future. There are many decisions which require careful analysis of pros and cons. Even people with the best of judgment may find difficulty in taking these decisions and thus they are tempted to approach the astrologer for guidance and counselling. The main purpose of astrology is in the area of future planning, counselling and channelising of energies or efforts in the right direction. We see so many misfits around us who keep on putting their efforts in one direction and end up as mediocres in a totally different field. If only appropriate counselling had been given at a suitable time, the entire fabric of the existence of any person may be different. Here would come the question whether a person can change his destiny. Yes, by right action, effort or karmas. Destiny is never deterministic. Our present karmas can change our destiny within the scope of adridha karma phala.

## The scope and limitations of prashna

The scope of prashna kundali extends to the events pertaining to near past or future and may extend upto a maximum of one year. There are several other limitations like fixing of Lagna which are dealt in detail in the following pages.

## Qualities of an Astrologer

Vraha Mihira in Brihat Samhita has given the characteristics of an astrologer which among others include the deep knowledge of mathematical and predictive astrology, a religious and righteous disposition, truthful, humble, disciplined, austere and having faith in God. Maharishi Vyas in Mahabharata has stated that every one takes birth as a shudra. It is his subsequent karmas which make him either a brahmin, kshatriya, vaishya or he remains a shudra. In our Hindu society even malechhas or yavanas having immense knowledge of religious scriptures or jyotisha have been held in high esteem as brahmins. One born as a brahmin and having the qualities narrated above is sure to command the respect of public as an astrologer.

The above narrated qualities include humility, austerity and faith in God. This faith in God imparts the divine grace to an astrologer. Association or aspect of Jupiter on 2H or 2L in a horoscope is a gift of God to any astrologer. 2H is the vaaksthana or the house of speech. Without the divine grace of vaaksiddhi through Jupiter's association or aspect, no astrologer can correctly predict even though he may be well versed in astrology. An astrologer should be a follower of austere living and thinking, not having any intention to cheat, and be well versed in veda, mantra and tantra. The entire conduct of prashna and its outcome depends upon the divine grace, which is a gift of God to the astrologer. For this he must have an unstinted faith in God and must follow a religious life. Any word spoken by such an astrologer can not go wrong. The central theme of qualities described for an astrologer are devotion and humility. There is a tendency among some modern astrologers to indulge in self praise, whereas the traditional knowledge of astrology has developed through humility and anonymity. Take the case of Nauka the commentary on Dashadhyayee, or for that matter Prashna Marga or Krishneeyam. The authors of these celebrated works had chosen to withhold their names. They dedicated their entire work to the divine. It has been the tradition of our ancient scholars to pass on the credit of their writings to their guru, deity or the supreme God, and hold themselves responsible for any mistakes and misgivings in the text. Such was the level of humility which we have inherited with this divine knowledge. What can an astrologer claim as an achievement of his prediction unless it is willed by God. This is the essence of our classical knowledge and guru shishya parampara in jyotisha. I think we, as astrologers, have to not only adopt this philosophy of humility as a way of life, but also show and teach it for others to emulate.

## How to approach an astrologer

A querist must have an urge to ask the prashna to alleviate those anxieties which are troubling him at that moment. He must select an auspicious tithi, nakshatra etc. and approach the astrologer in the morning after paying due obeisance to God, his Ishtdevata or Sthana devata as the case may be. The present day querist may not be aware of auspicious tithi, nakshatra etc. and even an inauspicious moment will also reveal the outcome of prashna. Hence in the modern context, a querist must approach an astrologer truthfully and sincerely, without any intention to either test or make fun of the astrologer. A querist should go to an astrologer, preferably in the morning, taking some fruits and flowers as his offering, facing or standing in the east direction, with folded hands, praying to God, ask one question, which is his foremost anxiety at that moment. The purpose of making an offering to the astrologer must be understood. The underlying idea is that a querist should not approach an astrologer empty handed. Any act done by an individual is part of kriyamaan karma, which gets accumulated as sanchita and manifests as prarabdha. If an astrologer is creating some karma by his effort, the load of its debt should not be carried over by a querist and hence some offering even in the form of flowers or fruits must be made. In the same context it is said that a querist who goes to an astrologer empty handed, returns from there empty handed.

## Sincere or insincere queries

A person who is not sincere in his approach, or who does not have an earnest desire to know the future to alleviate his sufferings, should not be entertained by the astrologer. Some of the combinations of an insincere query seen in the prashna chart are :

1. When Moon is posited in Lagna, Saturn is in a kendra and Mercury is combust.

2. Moon in Lagna aspected by Mercury and Mars.

3. 7L aspected by Jupiter, Mercury or a planet which is inimical to the 7L, without any benefic association.

These combination reveal an insincere query. There may also be a query from a person asking whether his prashna is sincere or not. In that case a benefic in Lagna indicates a sincere query and a malefic in Lagna indicates that the person is not sincere. Honesty of querist is also indicated if Lagna or the 7H are aspected by Moon, Jupiter and Mercury. Udaya Lagna and Arudha Lagna becomes same in sincere queries. Similarly in sincere queries generally, Lagna of prashna coincides with Lagna or Moon of birth chart.

What should an astrologer do when some unscrupulous person approaches him to test or make fun of astrology. If some one asks a prashna casually, without respect, using an abusive language then he may not be answered and may be asked to come some other time.

## Conducting the Prashna

The actual conduct of a prashna is a very spiritual phenomenon and for its auspicious and sacred performance the astrologer must follow the divine procedure as quantified in our classical texts. In the morning he must propitiate the navgrahas, the nine planets, and after offering obeisance to the almighty, facing east, light an oil lamp with a flame and wait for the querist to arrive. The astrologer must face the east direction while doing prashna because the flow of energies in this direction gives a lot of creativity.

The agni or flame has, since times immemorial been considered important in various shodash karmas or the sacred sixteen rituals connected with Hindu belief and religion. Agni is the medium of worship in vedic rituals and is considered as a means to carry or convey our offerings and prayers to the almighty.

The lamp used during the conduct of prashna has a divine symbolism and indicates the future. The container which contain the oil of the lamp indicates the home of the querist, the oil indicates the body, the wick indicates the soul and the flame indicates the longevity. The brightness or uniformity of flame indicates the happiness and good future whereas flickering or dim flame indicates miseries and illness. A smooth flowing, bright, quite and steady flame, which is having a pleasant colour and is straight, indicates prosperity to the querist. On the other hand a flame which is not bright and is flickering and also divided into two different parts indicates inauspicious and adverse results. Similarly if the flame is put out suddenly in spite of sufficient oil or the flame does not burn in spite of repeated efforts indicates evil as far as longevity is concerned. Flies in the oil indicate worms in the body. If two wicks are tied together then there are respiratory problems. A wick mixed with dirty or unclean articles like hair indicates that the person is suffering from troubles due to evil karmas or spirits. The lamp from which oil is oozing out or spilling from the brim indicates abundant water as in the case of prashnas related to water resources. The direction towards which the flame tilts due to natural flow of breeze or the condition of wick may also reveal evils. Generally the flame towards south, south-east or south-west directions is inauspicious.

| Flame | Indication |
|---|---|
| Moving anti-clockwise | Troubles |
| Towards south-east | Accident by fire |
| Towards south | Death |
| Towards south-west | Mental illness or *unmad* |

In the earlier times the astrologer used to do consultations and counselling in the open or under a tree where the breeze made the flame to react in a sober or violent way and thereby indicated the smooth or uncertain future respectively. However the present day astrologers do consultations in the closed walls of rooms where neither breeze nor natural omens are able to help the astrologer in his readings. In the earlier days if the querist brought his own lamp, the size and quality of the vessel or lamp indicated the quality and size of his house. Further if he moved his lamp, then it foretold his movement or change of house. But now no querist brings any lamp and hence these indications, though of immense value in the earlier days, have lost their importance.

These traditional techniques require certain modifications in the present times which have been dealt along with the chapter on omens. Nevertheless the importance of the oil lamp and the flame can not be ignored in spite of the fact that these days electric lights are used. An oil lamp with a flame placed next to the astrologer adds divine grace to the conduct of prashna. It has been observed that fluctuations in electricity denotes obstacles. Similarly if electricity goes off suddenly, the desired results of the prashna may not be achieved. There are places where no electric fluctuations or failures occur and hence this example of electricity is only indicative to show that any unnatural event occurring during the conduct of the prashna reveals like nimitta or omens. Hence the literal meaning of the old classical texts may have to be replaced in certain contexts by more liberal viewpoints and results interpreted accordingly.

## Self Prashna

It is often argued whether an astrologer should analyse a self prashna or not. Whenever any unfavourable combination like tithi, nakshatra etc. is running, an astrologer in tempted to put off his query. This should be avoided. As long as a truthful and sincere approach is resorted, self prashna is useful.

## Importance of Lagna and Arudha

In case of a birth horoscope the time, place and date of birth determines the chart of heavens in which the native takes birth. Quite similar to this is the birth of the thought, process or idea in which the prashna is asked, which reveals the outcome of prashna. The scope of the birth chart is the full life span of the native even though Maharishi Parashara suggests the life before and after death of the present life of the native can also be seen from the birth chart. But in the absence of any proofs and the possibility of research in this regard, these indications have largely remained ignored. On the other hand the scope of the prashna kundali is limited to the immediate past and immediate future of the native. The thought and anxieties crossing the mind of the person asking the prashna and the analysis of chart vis-a-vis predictions are generally limited to a period of about one year. In the birth chart, the rising sign becomes the most important pivot around which the entire fabric of a horoscope is knit. Similarly in prashna also the rising sign or Udaya Lagna or just Lagna, as we will refer to it hereafter, becomes most important. The Lagna, both in natal chart and prashna chart is the body of the native whereas the placement of Moon is his mind, or the inclination towards which his desires are directed. In the older times, the astrologers were mainly rishis or saints who were totally devoted to the cause, observations, study and research of astrology. Any observation leads to hypothesis, which when applied to thousands of horoscopes, starts giving replicable results and then these hypotheses turn into principles. Any scheme for acquisition of wisdom or gyana follows three steps. These are adhyayana, chintana and manana or the learning, deduction or reflection and contemplation. Even today if any astrologer wishes to reach a level of proficiency, then his mind has to follow the steps leading to chintana and manana. Only then the wisdom of divinity dawns upon the astrologer. These rishis of yore were not men with worldly ties or families and had no desires like our modern day astrologers. They had all the time of the day or night devoted for consultations. More so in the rajgharanas, the king could summon the Raj Jyotishi at any time and ask the prashna which was haunting his mind at that particular moment. For all such occasions the Udaya Lagna like the birth Lagna revealed the wealth of information to unfold the mysteries of future.

Let us come to the situation of the present day astrologers who are family men, with all their desires and failings. These astrologers are engaged in some service, profession or business and therefore have just no time to devote for consultations and serious pursuits of astrology. At best they are able to devote hardly a few hours in the evening when the Udaya Lagna may remain the same for nearly one month for those two hours or so. Do we mean to say that the results for all those people approaching the astrologer for full one month will be the same? In all such cases Arudha Lagna becomes essential.

In the earlier times two Lagnas the Udaya and the Arudha were calculated. Arudha is a Lagna other than the rising Lagna and is calculated by several methods. The relationship between udaya Lagna lord and arudha Lagna lord is called sutra. If these two are friends then it gives good results, if enemy then bad and if neutral then average results. In Jaimini astrology, the distance between the Lagna and the lord of Lagna when further counted from the position of the placement of Lagna lord becomes Arudha of Lagna. For example if Lagna lord is placed in the third house, then counting three from the third house, we come to the fifth house, which is termed as Arudha. This method employed in Jaimini has no role in prashna.

In the earlier times the Arudha Lagna was fixed from the direction occupied by the person asking the prashna. In the present times the astrologer is not sitting in the open under a tree where the direction from which the person approaches may be the determinant for Arudha Lagna. These days astrologer is confined to a small room where there is no scope for the querist to chose his seat or direction. Thus with the changing times the procedures for determining the Arudha Lagna have also changed. As stated above the importance of Arudha Lagna has assumed greater importance in the present times. Yet, whenever a person comes to the astrologer solely for the purpose of consultation and without seeking an appointment and not following the fixed hours of modern day astrologers, Udaya Lagna gives the most satisfactory and amazing results. Very often the above stated conditions are not fulfilled and hence the astrologer has to depend on the Arudha Lagna.

## Chatra Lagna

In addition to Udaya Lagna and Arudha lagna, another important Lagna is Chatra Lagna. Calculation of Chatra Lagna or rashi involves the following three steps.

### Step 1. Calculation of veethi rashi

If Sun is in Taurus, Gemini, Cancer or Leo i.e. the signs 2, 3, 4 and 5 the veethi rashi is Aries or Mesha. If Sun is in Scorpio, Capricorn or Aquarius i.e. signs 8, 10 or 11 the veethi rashi is Gemini. If Sun is in any of the remaining signs Aries, Virgo, Libra, Sagittarius or Pisces i.e. signs 1, 6, 7, 9 or 12 the veethi rashi is Taurus, this can be tabulated as follows :

| Sun in signs | Veethi rashi |
|---|---|
| 2, 3, 4, 5 | 1 (Aries) |
| 1, 6, 7, 9, 12 | 2 (Taurus) |
| 8, 10, 11 | 3 (Gemini) |

### Step 2

Ascertain at the time of prashna the rising Lagna as well as Arudha Lagna. Count from Arudha to rising Lagna. Say Arudha is Sagittarius and rising Lagna is Pisces, then counting from Sagittarius to Pisces we get 4. Call it x

### Step 3

Count from veethi rashi obtained in step 1 the number obtained in step 2. Say in a chart Sun is in Aries making the veethi rashi to be Taurus. Now count 4 (as in the above example in step 2) from Taurus to get Leo.

Thus Leo becomes chatra lagna. It is believed that strongest of Udaya Lagna, Arudha Lagna and Chatra Lagna gives best results in prashna. Yet in view of the factors explained above, a decision between Udaya and Arudha Lagna is to be taken. Chatra Lagna has a specific use in prashna which has been dealt at appropriate places in this book. However Chatra Lagna has been given identical importance to other Lagnas in classical texts. This fact should not be ignored and yet its use needs to be further tested and researched.

### 2. The gold or touch

Whenever a person does not ask a prashna from any particular direction, or when the direction as above is difficult to determine, for example when a query is raised while walking or moving up or down, then he is asked to place a piece of gold or just touch the chart at any point. The procedure is to prepare a chart on a clean spot, cover it with water, sandal paste, grains, rice and flowers. Then only he is asked to place the piece of gold or touch a spot, thereby indicating a rashi which will be Arudha Lagna. The gold piece represents the jeeva and the other articles used in preparation of the chart, as above, represents the deha. These represent the five elements the body is made up of. When gold piece is found to be in contact with these five materials then by combination of jeeva and deha, the querist lives long. When the gold piece and these five materials are not in contact then the longevity is reduced. Meagre quantity of these materials indicate that the constitution of the querist is weak and subject to attack of infections. When these five materials are contaminated then sickness of querist is indicated. In the present day conduct of prashna a gold ring, gold coin or any small gold ornament can be used.

In this method also the navamsha of Arudha can be found out by what is known as Arudha Navamsha chakra. The touch on any part of the chakra or placing the piece of gold on the particular smaller square determines the navamsha of Arudha.

### 3. The Ashtamangala prashna

It is a very elaborate system of prashna especially followed in the state of Kerala. As the name suggests it involves the use of eight articles which are lamp, gold, mirror, curd, milk, fruits, book and white cloth. Other related articles used in the worship or puja are white flowers, sandal paste, plantain leaves, rice etc.

The eight articles symbolise Goddess Saraswati, the goddess of learning, the lamp symbolise Lord Ganesha the God of intelligence and the flame symbolise Goddess Lakshmi, the Goddess of fortune and prosperity. The puja using these articles symbolises the invocation of these deities for the auspicious conduct of the prashna to light the path of the unknown and favourable fulfillment of the queries. The chart is drawn on a wooden plank with rice powder, having a four petal lotus in the center.

Gold coin represents jeeva and the other articles represent deha. Their mixing and subsequent analysis indicates the persons health and longevity. An elaborate puja is performed in which the zodiacal signs, planets, mandi and their subordinate deities are worshipped. The gold coin and 108 cowries are purified by washing in holy water and mixed with sandal paste, flowers and rice. These 108 cowries are worshipped for planets, nakshatras (their being a total of 108 nakshatra padas) and ancestors to reveal the unknown and for the divine sanction for looking into the future.

A young child is asked to worship the lamp being the embodiment of Gods. He is asked to place the gold coin, flowers and rice on any one sign of the chart after taking its parikrama and facing the East. This gives us the Arudha sign. All along the querist must stand, worshiping and thinking about the object of his query with devotion, continuing to meditate.

The astrologer takes out a handful of cowries and places them in front of him to his left, another handful in the middle and the remaining to his right. The prevalent omens and examination of breath are noted. The cowries kept at three places are noted ignoring the multiples of eight and considering the remainders only. There are two ways of analysing this. Firstly the number which is on the left side is the unit of hundreds, the number in the front is the unit of tens and the number on the right is the unit of ones. This three digit number is known as ashtamangala figure and is used for various readings. The hundredth digit denotes the past, the tens digit present, and the ones digit future. Secondly these numbers at these three places represent the planets.

| Remainder | Planet | Remainder | Planet |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sun | 5 | Venus |
| 2 | Mars | 6 | Saturn |

### Method 1: Based on planets

In determining Arudha, observe the first word spoken by the querist and to which planet it belongs. The letters placed at odd positions 1, 3, 5 denote odd signs of the planet and letters placed at even positions denote even signs of the planet. This is applicable for all planets except Sun and Moon which own one sign each.

Suppose a person utters chalo then the ruling planet for cha is Venus. Being the first letter in the table, an odd number, the Arudha will be odd sign owned by Venus i.e. Libra. If the first letter spoken by the querist denotes a benefic planet then favourable results follow, whereas a letter denoting a malefic planet gives unfavourable result.

## The modern methods of determining Arudha

### 1. By number 108

In the present era where neither the astrologer nor the querist has the time and together with limitations referred to earlier in using Lagna in many cases, Arudha is fixed by asking any number between 1 and 108. Divide this number by 9. The quotient denotes the signs which have already elapsed. Add one to the quotient to give the Arudha sign. The remainder indicates the rising navamsha. Let us take an example. Say the number spoken is 57. Dividing 57 by 9 we get the quotient 6 and the remainder 3. Add one to 6 giving 7 or Libra as the Arudha Lagna with third navamsha running. This means that Arudha Lagna is Libra with 6°40′ to 10° as its degrees. Based on this the navamsha Lagna can be calculated.

### 2. By touch

Make a horoscope chart preferably in the south Indian style without indicating the starting point or the Aries sign. But this starting point of Aries sign may be kept in mind by the astrologer. The person is asked to touch any one sign in the chart which will be Arudha Lagna. To determine the navamsha the Arudha navamsha chakra can be used. ### 4. By number 249

This method is expounded by K.S. Krishnamurti. There are 27 nakshatras in the zodiac each having an extent of 13°20′. These nakshatras are ruled by 9 planets in the order of Vimshottari dasha of Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn and Mercury in three triads of 9 nakshatras each. The ownership of different planets over different nakshatras have been given in the chapter on general principles of astrology. The method of determining Arudha involves asking the person any number between 1 and 249. Then refer to the tables given here to ascertain the Arudha sign based on this number. It also shows the influence of three planets on the resultant prashna chart, those of the sign lord, the nakshatra lord and the sub lord.

As per this method, Lagna or any planet posited in a horoscope has three major influences on it. The sign where a planet is posited, influenced by its lord, the nakshatra in which it is posited again influenced by its lord and finally the sub lord calculated on the basis of proportionate Vimshottari dasha for finer influences. In this scheme although all the three have an influence on the prashna concerned yet the sign lord, the nakshatra lord and the sub lord have their influence on the prashna in the increasing order.

Suppose in a prashna chart the sub lord is posited in the house of significator of the prashna, aspected by benefics then the prashna would lead to favourable results. Whereas the sub lord in an inauspicious house in the influence of malefics or 6, 8, 12 lords, karya siddhi would be difficult to achieve. By this method not only the Arudha is fixed for subsequent placement of planets in the chart, but also the additional influences of these three very important facets come to focus which eventually have a deciding impact on the very outcome of prashna.
