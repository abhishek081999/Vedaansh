Why check Bhava Bala before the planet?

Long image

A

Answer

House first, then sign, then graha. A strong planet on a weak house is talent without a platform — results in that life area stay hard to unlock.

  1. Bhava Bala scores the house as charged space; without it, Shadbala alone overstates what can happen.1
  2. On Vedaansh, totals are Rupas (totalRupa); lecture bands often quote Virupas (×60).2
  3. Raman-style floor used in code: ≥ 7.5 Rupas (450 Virupas) counts as strong.2
  4. Relative origin vs destination strength decides who dominates when a lord moves house.1

What is Bhava Bala?

Bhava Bala is house strength. It folds the lord’s Shadbala, the house’s digbala, and aspect pressure on the cusp into one score per house.2

Units

Lecture audio often says 400, 450, 500 — that is Virupas. Vedaansh Bhava tab shows Rupas. Divide Virupas by 60, or multiply Rupas by 60. 7.5 R = 450 V; 6 R = 360 V; 9 R = 540 V.

How is the score built?

Diagram structure
  • Adhipati Bala adip
  • Bhava Digbala dig
  • Bhava Drishti dri
  • adip → dig
  • dig → dri
  1. Adhipati Bala ↗

    Sign lord’s total Shadbala (Virupas) for that house’s Rashi.

  2. Bhava Digbala ↗

    Sign category vs zero-kendra; arc ÷ 3, max 60 Virupas.

  3. Bhava Drishti ↗

    Sputa aspects on the cusp; Ju/Me full; others ±¼; nodes out.

  1. Open Bhava Bala tab ↗

    Chart → Bhava Bala; read totals and strongest/weakest.

  2. Rank twelve house totals ↗

    Note peaks near 9R+ and dips under ~6R.

  3. Mark weak vs strong ↗

    Code: ≥7.5R strong. Lecture soft: 400–550+ Virupas = charged.

  4. Trace house lords ↗

    For each topic house, find its lord and that lord’s own Shadbala.

  5. Origin vs destination ↗

    Compare origin house strength to the house where the lord sits.

  6. Vastu or house remedies ↗

    Weak house → direction/Vastu plus house-specific remedial acts.

5

Origin and destination

When a house lord leaves House A for House B: A is supply (origin), B is demand (destination).

PatternTypical read
Strong origin → weak destinationUpper handLord dominates the landing house
Weak origin → strong destinationDemand winsDestination themes press the native
Strong 1st lord in weak 8thPower tiltNative can exploit / hold upper hand
Strong 8th lord in weak 1stOverwhelmNative absorbs crisis / humiliation risk

Relative, not absolute

A high origin number alone does not guarantee comfort in the destination. Compare both houses, then the planet’s own Shadbala. 1st–8th examples are diagnostic patterns, not moral labels.
