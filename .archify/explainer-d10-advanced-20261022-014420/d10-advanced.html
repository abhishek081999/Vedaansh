The upgrade matches classroom Digpala practice: lagna Digpala → 10th-lord Digpala → 11th-lord dasha → 8th ethical track, plus direction and profession sketch.

  1. Digpalas carry professions, compass advice, and prediction hooks — not one-line labels.

  3. Workspace tabs cover Advanced, Direction, Profession, Ethics, and Dasha × D10.
  4. Gandhi-style worked biography is not hard-coded; the same protocol runs on every chart.
