# Multi-varga Circuit — D3 → D7 → D10 → D9 for why events happen {#d10-circuit}

Vedaansh’s **Multi-Varga Circuit** tab reads relationship and career “why” questions across **D3 mind, D7 who enters, D10 karma, D9 fruit** (plus D2 for money/maternity) — because D10 alone can misread pregnancy as promotion.

- Sequence: D1 context → D3 thoughts → D7 partners → D10 actions → D9 destiny.
- Sync verdict: trust a theme only when several divisions agree on the running dasha.
- Job “cutter” (D10 8th) needs Mars/Saturn/Mercury/Sun flavor plus D3/D7 to name research vs exit vs new venture.
