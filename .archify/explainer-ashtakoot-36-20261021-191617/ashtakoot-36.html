Why did Kundali Milan call 36/36 “not good”?

Long image

A

Answer

A full 36/36 Ashtakoot score is excellent. The UI had framed scores above 32 as “caution / too similar,” which made a perfect match look bad.

  1. Eligibility used the label Very high gunas (caution) for every total above 32, including 36.
  2. The compare page put “Very high total” in the red Areas for awareness list.
  3. Strengths still said the score sat in the classical 18–32 window whenever traditionallyAccepted was true.
