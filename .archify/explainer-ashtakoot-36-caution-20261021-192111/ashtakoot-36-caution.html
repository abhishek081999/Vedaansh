Why is 36/36 Ashtakoot shown with caution?

Long image

A

Answer

A 36/36 gun match is mechanically maxed, but the product treats it as caution: totals above 32 are often “too similar,” and same Nadi remains a progeny concern even when an exception restores points.

  1. Ideal counselling band is 18–32 gunas.
  2. Above 32 (including 36) uses a caution verdict and gold eligibility band.
  3. Same Nadi never counts as a strength; cancelled same-Nadi still appears under Areas for awareness.
