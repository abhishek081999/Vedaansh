Why does Bhava Bala come before the planet?

Long image

A

Answer

House → Rashi → Graha. If the house is weak, strong Shadbala and a clean sign still cannot unlock that life area — the platform is uncharged.

  1. High Bhava Bala (~400–550+ Virupas) means fewer snags; effort in that area pays.1
  2. Low Bhava Bala means the space lacks energy to support outcomes.1
  3. House = platform; planet = talent — diagnose which one failed.1
  4. On Vedaansh, read Rupas (totalRupa); ×60 to match lecture Virupas.2

Origin supply vs destination demand

When a house lord leaves House A for House B: A = supply (origin), B = demand (destination). Origin strength travels with the lord; relative gap decides who dominates.

PatternRead
Strong origin → weak destinationUpper handLord dominates landing house
Weak supply vs high demandDemand winsDestination themes win
Strong 4th lord into 9thComforts may support higher path if 9th can receive
Weak 6th into strong 9thCannot balanceJob supply cannot fund heavy study demand

Worked numbers from the lecture

Strong 7th (700) lord into weak 3rd (300): 7th energy performs well in the 3rd. Strong Lagna lord into weak 8th: native holds upper hand (exploit tilt). Strong 8th lord into weak Lagna: crisis energy overwhelms the native (victim tilt). Always compare both houses, then the planet’s own Shadbala.

1st–8th is relative power

These are diagnostic tilts from relative Bhava Bala, not moral labels. Confirm lord placement and dasha before prediction.
