Answer

A new Adv tab deepens Dashamsha with dispositor chains, graha drishti, natal Shadbala and SAV, chara karakas in D10, raja links, and a career-tagged Vimshottari timeline.

  1. Dispositors show who feeds the 10th, lagna, and livelihood lords in D10.1
  2. Full and special aspects on the D10 10th and lagna name who colours status.2
  3. Strength and timing stay natal: Shadbala, bhava bala, SAV, and upcoming antars marked when a career lord runs.3

  1. Dispositor chain ↗

    Trace 10th, lagna, and livelihood lords through D10 sign lords.

  2. Drishti on 10th ↗

    Count Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn special aspects as full.

  3. Kendra–trikona links ↗

    Mark conjunction, exchange, or aspect between career house lords.

  4. Shadbala rows ↗

    Use natal ratios; weak lords still need dasha support.

  5. SAV on H10 H11 ↗

    High bindus ease transit and dasha fruit in those houses.

  6. Career-hit antars ↗

    Flag periods whose lord is a D10 career significator.
