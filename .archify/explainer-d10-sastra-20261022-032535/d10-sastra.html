How does the D10 Śāstra tab read a career?

Long image

A

Answer

The Śāstra tab names the kind of work from the birth chart, then reads Dashamsha for the status that work can reach.

  1. Phaladeepika scores the 10th lord, occupants, aspectors, and the Navamsha lord of the 10th lord.1
  2. BPHS treats Dashamsha as mahatphala, so the D10 10th lord’s house is the status result.2
  3. A yoga appears only when this chart forms it. Vimshottari of those lords times the result.3

How do you read one chart?

Diagram structure
  • Score the birth 10th d1
  • Place the D10 10th lord d10
  • Keep formed yogas yoga
  • Time those lords dasha
  • d1 → d10
  • d10 → yoga
  • yoga → dasha
  1. Score the birth 10th ↗

    Use the strongest of the four Phaladeepika pointers, plus the 10th from Moon and from Sun.

  2. Place the D10 10th lord ↗

    Read that lord’s Dashamsha house as the shape of status. Note if the 10th sign is movable, fixed, or dual.

  3. Keep formed yogas ↗

    Dharma-Karmadhipati, Digbala, Vargottama, Neecha Bhanga, and Budha-Aditya count only when formed.

  4. Time those lords ↗

    Watch Vimshottari of the 10th lord, the livelihood planet, and the Amatyakaraka.

Do not merge a split

If the livelihood planet and the D10 status lord differ, keep both. One names the kind of work. The other names how rank is earned.
