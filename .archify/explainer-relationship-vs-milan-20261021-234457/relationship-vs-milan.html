Why Relationship content is not fully on Kundali Matching

Long image

A

Answer

We can show most of it — and Milan already reuses the same married-life engines for both charts. The gap is product focus (pair match vs solo promise), not missing charts or calc.

  1. Relationship answers what one chart promises for married life.
  2. Kundali Matching answers how two charts fit, plus classical Ashtakoot and Porutham.
  3. Milan Married Life already dual-runs Jaya, D1/D9, houses, 7th lord, Venus–Jupiter, gochar, partner, separation, and karmic debt.
  4. A few Relationship-only tabs (intimate, combinations, Aries–Libra teaching, Ju/Ma/Sa notes) are still UI gaps, not engine gaps.

How to add the missing pieces

  1. Pick tabs to dual

    Intimate, combinations, Ju/Ma/Sa, and full 7th-axis teaching are the clear gaps.

  2. Reuse UI kit

    Mount the same section components from marriedLifeUi under dual columns.

  3. Add pair notes only where useful

    Cross-read when both charts speak to the same house or planet; otherwise show A vs B side by side.

  4. Keep Gold gate

    Match Relationship and existing Milan Married Life plan checks.

UI gap, not engine gap
