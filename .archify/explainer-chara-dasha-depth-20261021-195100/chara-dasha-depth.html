Why Chara Dasha showed only 2 levels for past and future

Long image

A

Answer

Chara (and Mandook/Sthir) only built level-3 Pratyantar under the current branch. Previous and future periods stopped at Antar (level 2). The builders now expand every branch to depth 3.

  1. buildSubDashas gated children on isCurrent, so non-current antars got empty children.
  2. DashaTree uses lazyExpand={false} for sign dashas, so it cannot fill missing levels on click.
  3. Hydrate reused cached Chara trees, so an old prune would stick until recalculate; it now always recomputes.
