What does the D10 Career Synthesis tab deliver?

Long image

A

Answer

One Career Synthesis tab now covers the full D10 roadmap: A10, profession card, job vs business, foreign, boss, Vimsopaka, Muntha year, D10 gochara, dasha windows, and a copyable brief.

  1. Public title comes from A10 (Rajya Pada); the work itself stays on the D10 10th.1
  2. Profession fields merge Phaladeepika, Digpala, and nakshatra sources with an agreement score.2
  3. Timing uses Tajika Muntha on D10 houses, live gochara from /api/transits/planets, and Vimshottari windows.3
