Vedaansh’s new Karma Yoga tab maps Bhagavad Gītā chapter 3 onto Dashamsha: 8th/12th dashas end work chapters; remedies align acceptance, not destiny.

  1. Dasha lords are read in D10 houses (8 = exit, 12 = release/faith, 5+8 = demotion, 5+12 = transfer, 11 = team shift).
  2. Fire/Earth/Air/Water house triads pick yajña-style remedies (agnihotra, dāna, kīrtan, pilgrimage).
  3. Strong-in-D1 / soft-in-D9 planets open D10 for the corrupting karma; D1↔D10 lagna sync flags career stability.
