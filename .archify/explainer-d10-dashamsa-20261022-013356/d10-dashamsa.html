Dashamsha (D10) is the career and livelihood chart

Long image

A

Answer

D10 divides each sign into ten 3° parts and shows profession, income source, status, and work karma — read with D1, Digpalas, and the 10th/11th lords.

  1. Odd signs count Dashamsha from the planet’s own sign; even signs start from the 9th.
  2. Narada’s ten Digpalas (Indra→Ananta) color how each 3° part earns and leads.
  3. Priority houses in D10: 1 (foundation), 10 (karma), 11 (gains), 6 (competition).
  4. Vedaansh ships a full-width Dashamsha workspace under Core Analysis.

How is D10 calculated?

Diagram structure
  • Degree in sign deg
  • Part 1 to 10 part
  • Odd sign? odd
  • From own sign same
  • From 9th sign ninth
  • D10 rashi d10
  • deg → part · 3° each
  • ninth → d10 · add parts
  • odd → ninth · no
  • odd → same · yes
  • part → odd
  • same → d10 · add parts
  1. Degree in sign ↗

    Take the planet’s degree within its 30° Rashi.

  2. Part 1 to 10 ↗

    Floor(degree / 3) + 1 gives the Dashamsha part.

  3. Odd sign? ↗

    Odd Rashis count forward from the same sign.

  4. D10 rashi ↗

    Even Rashis count from the 9th; engine D10() matches this.

Odd vs even Digpala order

Odd signs run Indra → Ananta across parts 1–10. Even signs reverse that order. Paired degree bands in some Narada teachings (e.g. Indra at 0–3° and 27–30°) describe the same deities at both ends of the sign.
